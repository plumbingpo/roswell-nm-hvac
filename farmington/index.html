Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Farmington

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement in peak season.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a miserable night.
