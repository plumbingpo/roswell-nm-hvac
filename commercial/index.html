Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff during the rush. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safety

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Fast Response

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Roswell HVAC provides fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Roswell and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, restore heating or cooling, and document the entire call for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place so the same failure doesn't return.
