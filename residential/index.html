Upfront Pricing

Straight Answers and Upfront Prices

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Roswell HVAC offers honest, upfront pricing across Roswell, covering everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a careful inspection of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a plain-English recommendation with the price spelled out before any work starts.

Many HVAC problems stay hidden until a failure is well underway. Refrigerant seeps out through pinhole leaks in the evaporator coil, blower bearings wear slowly, and a weakening capacitor gives no warning until the compressor won't start on the first hot day. If you notice weak airflow, rising energy bills, ice on the refrigerant lines, or rooms that never reach the thermostat setting, call for an inspection sooner rather than later. An early diagnosis often keeps a repairable issue from becoming a replacement.

Not every call is an emergency. Many Roswell homeowners call us to size up an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical AC or furnace lifespan of 12 to 18 years, replacing on your own schedule beats scrambling for a new unit during a cold snap. We'll give you a straight answer on how much life your equipment has left.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service prevents failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still inexpensive to fix. At Roswell HVAC, every plan is built around your home's specific equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check across the duct system.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and the plan is tailored to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

The savings show up quickly. Clean coils move heat more efficiently, a correct refrigerant charge takes strain off the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Compared with the cost of replacing a system even one year early, a maintenance plan is a bargain.

Right-Sized Systems

The Right Size System for Your Home

When it comes to HVAC, bigger is not better. An oversized unit short-cycles, burns extra energy, and never runs long enough to pull humidity out of the air, while an undersized one runs constantly and still falls behind on the hottest afternoons. We don't simply copy the tonnage of the old unit. Instead, we run a proper load calculation that factors in your square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork, and for mini-splits we size each head to the room it serves.

Choosing new equipment is about more than a SEER2 number. Our technician will compare furnace-and-AC setups with heat pumps, and ducted systems with ductless mini-splits, walking through operating costs, electrical and venting requirements, and the installation itself. Families with a growing household often do best with two-stage or variable-speed equipment, which holds tighter temperatures across the whole house and uses less energy. You choose what fits your home and budget.

Common Problems

Common HVAC Problems We Fix in Roswell

Homes in Roswell run into a familiar set of heating and cooling failures, and each one calls for a different fix. Roswell HVAC handles them all: no cooling, air that blows but never gets cold, furnaces that won't ignite or short-cycle, frozen evaporator coils, refrigerant leaks, condensers that hum without starting, thermostats that lose their settings, clogged condensate drains, and mini-splits flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Not every HVAC problem is equally urgent. A bad capacitor just keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can let combustion gases into the house, and a backed-up condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling before anyone notices. We come prepared to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, repair leaky ducts that dump conditioned air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
